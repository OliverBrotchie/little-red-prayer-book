\prayer{Introduction}

Prayer, according to Saint John Chrysostom, is a large and mighty weapon, a great possession, an instrument of the meeting of man with God; it is a medicine, a never-ending richness; it is the veins of the soul through which the grace of God flows freely, an invitation to eternal life. Prayer makes man like the Angels. It is man's conversation with God. `Nothing has its measure.' Nothing can compare with it, writes the holy Father. Prayer is a skill, and thus it requires practice and continued effort. It must be offered with a zealous soul and with deep concentration so that we realise what exactly we are doing, before Whom we stand and with Whom we are conversing. \textit{Pray without ceasing}, Saint Paul tells us. In joy, in sorrow, with patience, endurance, with contrition and humility.
